Cases, not chapters

The learning is in the argument. Harvard Business Publishing, Ivey and Darden sell single cases to individuals for roughly $9–11. Write your two-page recommendation before you read the teaching note.

Your own P&L is the cohort

An EMBA's real value is fifty classmates arguing from fifty balance sheets. Alone, substitute your employer: value it, model its unit economics, run every framework against it.

If you only do four

ACC 501, FIN 521, STR 531, NEG 561. Those four carry most of what an EMBA actually changes about how you work. The rest deepens; these four convert.

Sourcing the materials

Prior editions of the textbooks cost a fraction of current ones and differ trivially — buy used. Damodaran publishes his full NYU valuation course, slides and spreadsheets free at pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar. Filings come free from EDGAR.

The part you cannot buy

You can replicate the curriculum, not the room. Compensate deliberately: find three people who will read your memos and argue back, and present the capstone to them out loud. A recommendation nobody has attacked has not been tested.
